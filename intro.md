### 1. The Challenge We Face

- **Kubernetes is the most complex tool** in modern software engineering.
- **Amazon EKS is the most complex service** on AWS.

Our final goal is ambitious: we want to understand, build, and deploy a complete, scalable, production-grade system on AWS EKS.

Because our goal is so big, **we cannot achieve it all at once**. Trying to absorb everything in a single leap will only lead to feeling overwhelmed. To succeed, **we must split our goal into small, manageable steps**.

---

### 2. The Reality of Our Time Together

As you already know, **this is a very short class for a massive topic**.

Because our time is limited, it is simply impossible to teach every single detail, command, and edge case during this short period. 

Instead of trying to rush through endless theory, my goal is to:
- **Introduce the core concepts** clearly so you build the right mental models.
- **Guide you on how to learn step-by-step and effectively**, showing you the exact path senior engineers take.
- **Provide you with this working codebase**, giving you a production-grade template that you can explore, practice with, and return to long after this class ends.

---

### 3. How to Approach This Journey

1. **Take it one step at a time**: Resist the urge to jump straight to the cloud on day one. Master the basics first.
2. **Focus on intuition over memorization**: Understand *why* each layer exists — from Linux runtimes and Docker containers, to local Kubernetes clusters, to managed cloud infrastructure.
3. **Use this repository as your guide**: Treat this codebase as your personal roadmap and starter kit for your future projects.

I am excited to introduce you to Kubernetes and guide you through this journey. Let's take it step by step! 🚀
